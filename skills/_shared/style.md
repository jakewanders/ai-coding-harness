# Writing style

<!-- distinctive: Write for the reader who arrives cold and has ninety seconds. -->

Write for the reader who arrives cold and has ninety seconds.

- Lead with the outcome, then the evidence, then the detail.
- One idea per sentence. Prefer plain verbs over nouns made from verbs.
- Name files, functions and ids exactly as they appear; never invent shorthand.
- Numbers go in tables or on their own line, not buried in prose.
- Never claim something passed unless you ran it and saw the output. Quote the line that proves it.
- When something was skipped or could not be verified, say so first, in one sentence.
- No filler openings, no closing offers, no restating what you just did.
