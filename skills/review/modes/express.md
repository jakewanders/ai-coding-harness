# review — express mode

Express merges the specialist columns into one dispatch: you record every required column yourself in one pass.

- Record `criteria_forward`, `criteria_reverse`, `architecture`, `security`, `tests`, `performance`, `diffaudit`, `ledger` and `adversarial` as separate `review_column` records, even when several are empty; synthesis needs each column present.
- `reach_audit_recorded` and `counterweight_consulted` are warn in this mode: record what you can and note in your report what was not consulted.
- Synthesize once at the end with `evidence synthesize`; report the verdict.
